How VAT really works

VAT is an indirect consumer tax — entirely at the expense of the final consumer. For a company it is never an expense and never a revenue; it flows through.

Each company pays VAT to its supplier (reclaimable) and charges VAT to its customer (payable), then settles only the difference — the VAT on the value it added. The flat-screen-TV chain:

StagePurchaseVAT reclaimSaleVAT dueValue addedVAT to tax office
Manufacturer——1 0002101 000210
Wholesaler1 0002101 500315500105
Retailer1 5003151 90039940084
Consumer pays1 900————399

The stages remit 210 + 105 + 84 = 399 — exactly the VAT the consumer paid. The tax reaches the government in stages, but the consumer bears all of it.

The two VAT accounts411 VAT to reclaim = VAT paid to supplier → an asset (debit). 451 VAT due = VAT charged to customer → a liability (credit).

03

The VAT declaration

Periodically you settle with the tax office by closing the two VAT accounts against each other.

  1. Compare the balances of 411 and 451.
  2. Close the account with the smaller balance into the larger.
  3. Whatever remains is the net VAT payable (or receivable) — pay/collect it.

Example: 411 debit balance 210, 451 credit balance 315.

31/10 — declaration (DIV)
451VAT due210,00
@ 411VAT to reclaim210,00
07/11 — pay net VAT of 105 (C/A)
451VAT due105,00
@ 5500Bank account105,00

⭐ The complete scheme of an invoice

The single most important thing in the chapter. Every invoice is built from this ladder — learn the order, it never changes.

Price of the goods
− Commercial discount608 (buy) / 708 (sell)
+ Additional chargesinto 604 / 70
= NET amount invoice604 (buy) / 70 (sell)
− Financial discount757 / 657 — at payment only
= BASIS FOR VAT← VAT computed on THIS line
+ VAT411 (buy) / 451 (sell)
= TOTAL (if quick payment)
  TOTAL (if NO quick payment)440 (buy) / 400 (sell)
Three things everyone trips over 1. VAT is calculated on the “Basis for VAT” — after the financial discount — whether or not the discount is taken.
2. The financial discount is NOT booked at invoice time. Goods go in at NET; the supplier/debtor at the “if NO quick payment” total; the discount (757/657) appears only at payment.
3. Commercial discount is booked at invoice time (608/708); additional charges simply raise the net.

05

Commercial discount

A reduction on the price itself (quantity, promotion, damaged goods, valued customer). Relates to normal trading → operating result.

Purchase → 608 Discounts received (−) (reduces expense). Sale → 708 Discounts granted (−) (reduces revenue). Two acceptable methods:

Method A — separate (primary): goods at gross, discount separate.
604Purchases of GFR2 000,00
411VAT to reclaim399,00
@ 608Discounts received (−)100,00
@ 440Suppliers2 299,00
Method B — netted: book goods straight at net (1 900), no 608 line. Same VAT (399), same supplier total (2 299).
604Purchases of GFR1 900,00
411VAT to reclaim399,00
@ 440Suppliers2 299,00

10 smartphones @ 200, 5% discount → net 1 900; VAT 21% of 1 900 = 399.

06

Additional charges

Direct buying/selling costs on the invoice: transport, loading, insurance. They raise the net — so VAT applies to them too.

Purchase → operating expense ↑ (into 604). Sale → operating revenue ↑ (into 70).

Purchase: 20 computers @ 500 = 10 000; −5% = −500; +50 transport; net 9 550; VAT 2 005,50; total 11 555,50.

604Purchases of GFR9 550,00
411VAT to reclaim2 005,50
@ 440Suppliers11 555,50
07

Financial discount

A reduction if you pay within a time limit. It's about payment timing (a financing decision) → financial result.

Purchase → 757 Financial discounts received (financial revenue). Sale → 657 Financial discounts granted (financial expense).

Two golden rules1. VAT is calculated on the amount after the financial discount — even if never taken. 2. The discount is booked only at payment, and only if taken.

Purchase, 3% cash discount: 20 computers @ 500 = 10 000 net; disc. 300; basis 9 700; VAT 2 037; total-if-quick 11 737; total-if-not 12 037.

Invoice — goods at net, supplier at “no quick payment” total
604Purchases of GFR10 000,00
411VAT to reclaim2 037,00
@ 440Suppliers12 037,00
Payment within 10 days — discount now appears
440Suppliers12 037,00
@ 5500Bank account11 737,00
@ 757Financial discounts received300,00

If paid after 10 days: 440 D 12 037 / @5500 C 12 037. No VAT correction — VAT stays on the 9 700 basis. (Sales mirror: 70 at net, 400 at the “no quick payment” total; on collection debit 657.)

08

Credit notes

A mini reverse-invoice — a proportional correction of everything the original booked.

Incoming (ICN)Received from your supplier (you're the buyer): returned goods or extra discount → reverses a purchase.
Outgoing (OCN)You draw it up as the seller: customer returns goods or you grant extra discount → reverses a sale.
ICN — return 2 smartphones (net 380, VAT 79,80):
440Suppliers459,80
@ 604Purchases of GFR380,00
@ 411VAT to reclaim79,80
OCN — grant extra 5% (35, VAT 7,35):
70Sales of GFR35,00
451VAT due7,35
@ 400Trade debtors42,35

Modes of payment

Same event, different account per instrument. The accounting document tells you which.

InstrumentDocumentCollecting a salePaying a purchase
BankC/A extract5500 ↑ (D)5500 ↓ (C)
Cashcash document570 ↑ (D)570 ↓ (C)
Chequecheque540 ↑ (D)5501 ↑ (C)
The cheque two-stepA cheque isn't cash yet. Received: ① 540 D / 400 C, then ② when cleared 5500 D / 540 C. Written: ① 440 D / 5501 C, then ② when cashed 5501 D / 5500 C. Accounts 540 & 5501 bridge the gap until money actually moves at the bank.
10

Internal transfers of funds

Moving money between the company's own accounts. The two legs fall on different days, so bridge them with account 58.

Take €2 000 cash to the bank:

Day 1 — cash leaves the till (CD)
58Internal transfers of funds2 000,00
@ 570Cash at hand2 000,00
Day 2 — bank confirms deposit (C/A)
5500Bank account2 000,00
@ 58Internal transfers of funds2 000,00

After both entries, account 58 nets to zero — it existed only to bridge the two days.

Common mistakes

❌ Mistake✅ Fix / why
VAT on the net before the financial discountVAT is on the basis for VAT = net minus financial discount, taken or not.
Booking the financial discount at invoice timeOnly at payment (757/657); at invoice, goods at net, supplier/debtor at the “no quick payment” total.
Financial discount in the operating resultIt's financial: 757 revenue / 657 expense.
Commercial discount in the financial resultIt's operating: 608 / 708.
Correcting VAT when a cash discount is not takenNo correction — VAT stays on the original basis.
Treating a cheque as immediate cashBridge: 540 (received) / 5501 (written) until it clears at 5500.
Skipping account 58 on an internal transferThe legs fall on different days — 58 bridges and nets to zero.
Mixing 608 (credit) and 708 (debit)Received = buyer (credit 608); granted = seller (debit 708).
Recording a fixed-asset purchase hereA machine/van is an investment, not a commercial transaction.
